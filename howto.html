How To Use Steel Optimizer

A simple step-by-step guide to optimize your steel cutting process

Step 1: Prepare Your Order Data

Before using the calculator, gather the following information for each steel piece you need to cut:

  • Width: Choose from standard sizes (10, 13, 16, 19, 22, 25, 29, 32 mm)
  • Length: Specify the required length in millimeters
  • Quantity: Number of pieces needed

Step 2: Input Your Orders

  1. Navigate to the Calculator page
  2. Click "Add Row" to create a new order entry
  3. Select the width from the dropdown menu
  4. Enter the required length in millimeters
  5. Specify the quantity needed
  6. Repeat for all your orders (up to 10 rows maximum)

Step 3: Run Optimization

  1. Click "Optimize Cuts" button
  2. Wait for the algorithm to process (usually under 1 second)
  3. Review the optimization results

Tips for Best Results

📏 Accurate Measurements

Ensure all length measurements are precise to get optimal grouping results.

📊 Batch Similar Orders

Group orders with similar dimensions together for better optimization.

🔄 Consider Remainders

Save leftover pieces for future orders to maximize material utilization.

⚡ Regular Optimization

Run optimization regularly as new orders come in to maintain efficiency.
